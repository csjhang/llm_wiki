# Purpose

Output language: English

## Goal

This sample wiki shows how a Mechanism Wiki works. It holds three short articles from three fields. The goal is to find a mechanism that appears in more than one field, and to test whether it carries over from one field to another.

## Key questions

- Which mechanism do asynchronous circuits and spiking neural networks share?
- What does each field gain from this mechanism, and what does it cost?
- Does the same mechanism appear in franchise licensing, or is that field unrelated to it?
- What would a hypothesis that moves the mechanism from one field to another look like?

## Out of scope

- Detailed circuit design, chip layout, or timing analysis.
- Training code, benchmarks, or model comparisons.
- Legal advice on franchise contracts.
